About this Project About our Team

Our interdisciplinary research group brings together experts in Sinophone literature, corpus translation studies, language engineering, and generative AI. Led by academic faculty at the Department of Language Science and Technology at PolyU, our team works to analyze, digitize, and preserve classic Hong Kong literary titles for scholars and general readers world-wide.

Department of Language Science and Technology

The Department of Language Science and Technology at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University leads cutting-edge research in natural language processing, digital humanities, and translation technology. Through strategic academic grants, our faculty continues to pioneer AI-driven tools that honor local cultural nuance while adhering to global translation and archiving standards.

Our team members oversee corpus quality, align dual-language literary texts, and construct intuitive, highly accessible e-reading platforms for educational and archival research.
