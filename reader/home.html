About This Project

This platform serves as an interactive digital archive and translation repository dedicated to Hong Kong Sinophone Literature produced between 1900 and 1997. Powered by modern Generative AI translation frameworks and overseen by academic researchers at the Department of Language Science and Technology, PolyU, this initiative bridges cultural divides, preserving historical literary treasures while providing international scholars and modern readers with flexible digital e-reading tools.
